## The idea comes from Arcana Primária

The core move here, replacing Vancian slots with an accumulating fatigue that makes magic riskier
instead of impossible, came from the magic system of *Arcana Primária*, a Brazilian d20/OSR-flavoured
game by Nozes Game Studio. I read their system at a table, liked what it did to how my casters
behaved, and rebuilt the concept on PF2e's engine.

The borrowing is a mechanical concept, not text. Nothing from Arcana Primária is reproduced here: no
rules text, no tables, no values, no phrasing, no art, no setting material. The engine is also
completely different because it has to be: degrees of success, DC by rank, fatigue added to the
caster's own DC, categories measured from your maximum rank, Repetition Strain, Vital Overload, the
Spellstrike resolution. None of that is how their game works.

Mechanics are not the protected part of a game, expression is, so this probably isn't even a legal
question. I credit it anyway because it's someone's design and that seems like the decent way to share.

I read the license the studio publishes for its own system before writing this. Their SRD is CC BY-SA
and they are openly fine with people building on the rules, including commercially; what they ask for is
credit, no use of their brands or artwork, and no suggestion that an author endorses someone's project.
Two choices follow from that:

- The name *Arcana Primária* appears here only to identify whose design inspired this. No logo, no
  cover, no art, nothing styled like their product. If you would rather your mark did not appear in a
  stranger's repository, say so and I will write "a Brazilian OSR game" and change nothing else.
- Nothing in this repository suggests Nozes Game Studio knows about it, approved it, or shares
  responsibility for it. The offer above to change my wording is about my text, not a claim that we have
  talked, because we have not.

## The macros

Written by me. They call the public Foundry API and the pf2e system's own item APIs, and bundle no
assets, no images, no compendium content.

They deliberately follow the API pattern of the PF2e Workbench caster macros by Simon Althoff
(gitlab.com/symonsch/my-foundryvtt-macros), because that is the pattern that has kept working across
system versions. I read that code as documentation and wrote mine from scratch; these files are not a
fork and share no code with it. It is still the reason these macros work, so it gets the credit.

Foundry VTT is The Foundry Network's product. Nothing here is a module and nothing uses their assets.
